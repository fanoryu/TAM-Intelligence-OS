# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the TAM Intelligence OS project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act in ways that keep the project professional,
respectful, and productive.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy toward other contributors
- Handling finance, payroll, and personal data responsibly and confidentially

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sharing or exfiltrating real employee, payroll, contract, or bank data outside authorized channels
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities

The repository owner (**@fanoryu**) is responsible for clarifying standards and may take fair,
appropriate corrective action — including editing or removing contributions, and restricting
participation — in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, and
related communication) and when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner via GitHub. All reports will be
reviewed and handled with discretion. The owner will respect the privacy and security of anyone who
reports an incident.
